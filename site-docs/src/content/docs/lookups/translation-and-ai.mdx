For a line you can't read yet, the Log can translate it, explain it, or read it aloud. All three are
in each line's menu (hover a line and press **⋯**, or right-click it). Translations and
explanations are in English, whatever [language you're learning](/language/support/#translation-and-ai).

<Screenshot id="line-actions" alt="A line's menu in the Log: Copy text, Translate, Explain and Delete line." width={720} />

## Translate

**Translate** shows the line in English in place (**Show original** switches back); press
<kbd>T</kbd> while hovering a line to toggle it. Choose where translations come from in
**Settings → Translation & AI → Translate with**:

| Option | What it uses |
| --- | --- |
| **Kimochi cloud** | Kimochi's server (DeepL behind it). Needs [Kimochi Cloud](/cloud/); offered once you have it. |
| **Your DeepL key** | Your own [DeepL API](https://www.deepl.com/pro-api) key, entered below it. Only for languages DeepL translates from ([the list](/language/support/#languages)); for others, Settings says so. |

Until you choose, Kimochi uses Kimochi cloud if your account has Kimochi Cloud, and your DeepL key
otherwise.

Your DeepL key is kept in `settings.json` and isn't synced or backed up.

## Explain

**Explain** (or <kbd>E</kbd> on a hovered line) opens a grammar-focused explanation of the line,
starting with a one-line translation, using the lines before it as context. Answers are cached
(**Cached**); **Regenerate** runs it again with the current settings. You can Shift-hover words
inside the explanation too.

**Settings → Translation & AI → Explain with AI → Configure…** chooses who answers:

| Provider | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Codex CLI** (default) | Runs the `codex` command-line tool. Model: the CLI's default, or one you name. |
| **Claude CLI** | Runs the `claude` command-line tool. Model: Opus (default), Sonnet, Haiku or Fable. |
| **Kimochi cloud** | Kimochi's server, with Kimochi Cloud. |

The **Prompt** is yours to edit, with `{{language}}` (the language you're learning), `{{context}}`
(the previous lines) and `{{sentence}}` placeholders; **Reset to default** restores it. The default
prompt follows the language; one you edited is sent as written, and in a language other than
Japanese the dialog says so.

## Voice chat

When one or two things on screen are unclear, you can ask out loud. With text scanned on the
overlay, press the **Voice chat** shortcut (<kbd>Shift+F7</kbd>, <kbd>⇧⌘7</kbd> on macOS) and ask,
in English or whatever language you speak, about a word, a reading or a bit of grammar. An AI tutor
that knows the text on screen, where each part of it sits and the lines that were on screen before
it (a dialog box's earlier lines, say) answers in voice, and a card at the top of the screen shows what you said and its answer. You can interrupt it.

Point at what you mean: the tutor is told which word your pointer is on, and where it moves, so
"what does this mean?" is enough. It gets the text, not a picture of the screen.

Press the shortcut again to end, or just stop talking: the chat ends after about 25 seconds of quiet,
and after 3 minutes at most.

To set it up:

1. Add an [OpenAI API key](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys) in **Settings → Translation & AI →
   Voice chat**, and pick a voice. The chat uses OpenAI's GPT-Live, which OpenAI bills to that key
   by the second (separately from a ChatGPT subscription). The key is kept in `settings.json`
   and isn't synced or backed up.
2. Turn on the **Voice chat** shortcut in **Settings → Shortcuts & Controls**, where you can change
   its key too.

Use headphones: the microphone also hears your game's sound through speakers, and the tutor may
take a game's voice for yours. Your voice and the lines go to OpenAI only while a chat is on.

## Read aloud

:::note[Japanese only]
Read aloud's voices are Japanese, so it's hidden while you're learning another language.
:::

With [Kimochi Cloud](/cloud/), each line's menu has **Read aloud**, which plays the line in an
ElevenLabs voice. Choose the **Read-aloud voice** and **Voice model** (Eleven v3, v4 or v4 Turbo) in
**Settings → Log & Sessions → Reading**.
